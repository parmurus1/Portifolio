## Users

Two audiences, both confirmed by the owner:

- **Recruiters and tech leads** looking at a junior/early-career developer from São Paulo. They skim, open one or two live projects, then go to GitHub or LinkedIn.
- **Small-business clients** (a burger house, a rock band, a church, an English teacher: the kind of people he already builds for) who want a site and need to see real delivered work and a way to reach him.

## Product Purpose

Murilo Neves Teixeira's personal portfolio. It has to make both audiences think "this person ships real, playful, finished things", let them try the work, and reach him. Success = clicks to live projects, GitHub, LinkedIn, and contact.

## Positioning

## Product Principles

1. Show the work running, not described: every project is one click from being played or opened.
2. Programming is the material, not a costume: the site should behave like code doing something.
3. Personality over template: humour and play are proof of skill here, not decoration.
4. Fast and accessible despite the motion: static, light, reduced-motion respected.
